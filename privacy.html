Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

Summary: Ludo Clans does not require an account, does not collect personal identity information, and does not sell user data. Your game progression is stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Ludo Clans ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you install and play Ludo Clans on mobile platforms.

2. Information Collection & Storage

A. Personal Information

Ludo Clans does not require account registration, social network logins, email addresses, phone numbers, or real names to play. We do not maintain user identity databases on external servers.

B. Local Device Storage (Offline First)

Game progression, in-game virtual currency balances (Coins and Gems), unlocked Clan Heroes, match statistics, and audio/haptic preferences are stored solely on your local device using standard Android secure shared preferences. This data remains on your hardware and is never synced to external private servers without your explicit consent.

C. Automated Device Diagnostics

To guarantee stability across different Android devices and screen sizes, non-identifiable technical diagnostic data (such as OS version, device model, and crash reports) may be collected automatically via official Google Play Console services.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To provide optional gameplay rewards, Ludo Clans integrates trusted third-party SDKs that operate in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used exclusively for voluntary rewarded video ads (e.g., watching a brief video to claim bonus in-game currency). We strictly prohibit intrusive interstitial ads during active matches. Google may collect and process device advertising identifiers in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: If virtual items are purchased, payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Ludo Clans is a family-oriented tabletop board game built for players of all ages. In full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child protection regulations:

  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • No chat features, social profile links, or open user communications exist within the app.
  • Advertisements served adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

Because all gameplay data is retained on your local device, you hold 100% control over your data:

  • Instant Data Deletion: You may permanently erase all saved progress, coin balances, and settings at any moment by navigating to Settings → Apps → Ludo Clans → Clear Storage / Clear Data on your Android device, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Ad Personalization Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

6. Security

We take the protection of your local data seriously. The application operates within the Android operating system's application sandbox, preventing unauthorized cross-app access to local configuration files.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements in game features or statutory regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
